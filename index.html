The short version

An operator who learned to build.

My background is mixed on purpose. Here's how the pieces fit.

2016 – 2017

It started at General Assembly.

I learned full-stack web development in General Assembly's immersive program — React, Node, the whole stack — and built my first real projects there, including a side-scrolling game with art and audio I made myself. The catch: I did the entire thing while bartending three to four nights a week. That's where the code story began.

2018 – 2024

A decade in the trenches of operations.

I went into hospitality and spent years as a general manager of high-volume bars and live-music venues in Austin — teams of 100+, P&L, inventory, vendors, and the daily chaos of keeping a real business running. I learned exactly how operations actually work, and precisely where they bleed time and money.

2025 → now

AI changed what one operator could build.

I came back to code — this time with Claude, Cursor, and a decade of operational scar tissue. Now I ship real products fast. I'm not a developer who read about business. I'm an operator who learned to build the tools I always wished I had.
