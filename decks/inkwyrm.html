IV · The Journey
How It Works

From "I want a story" to pressing play in five minutes flat.

i

Onboard

A quick survey. Tap your favorite genres, books, films, and characters from a searchable library.

ii

Profile

The app builds a Taste Profile — themes, tone, intensity, the archetypes you keep returning to.

iii

Brief

Tell it the moment. Long drive? Sleepless night? Set length, lead, mood, intensity, perspective.

iv

Generate

A custom story is written, narrated in the voice you chose, scored if you want immersive audio.

v

Publish

Love it? Push it to the Inkwyrm shelf. Earn from reads, build a following, get noticed.
