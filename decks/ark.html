03 // IN THE DUST

You ask.
It answers.

// An embedded engine reads your situation, your terrain, your surroundings — and returns the most relevant knowledge from its bundled archive.

USER INPUT

"I'm in Austin, Texas. Grid's down. I need food and water. There's an abandoned house nearby with no power. What do I do?"

ARK // RESPONSE

Central Texas — your area: prickly pear cactus pads (cooked), dandelion greens, mesquite pods. Avoid water hemlock near streams.

Water: collect rain, look for springs in limestone areas, boil 1+ minute. Cattail roots near water = both food and a hydration indicator.

That abandoned house: copper wiring is salvageable for trade or repair. Check for a water heater — 40+ gallons of drinkable water inside if the grid just failed. Roof rainwater collection is your fastest setup.
